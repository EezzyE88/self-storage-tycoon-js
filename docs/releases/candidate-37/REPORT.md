## Result

A valid won, acknowledged Comeback property participates in career without losing its scenario record or market rules. One pure helper supplies eligibility and eligible portfolio totals. Active/other authored scenarios, creative properties and unfinished tutorials neither contribute nor receive new career tiers. Existing sandbox options, including configured tier overrides, remain intact.

Promotion runs hourly after all properties finish the minute. Selecting an excluded property cannot block other eligible properties or promote the challenge. Earned tier never decreases; acquired eligible properties inherit the earned company level on a normal hourly checkpoint. Excluded saved tiers are retained but cannot propagate. Career roll is rounded to monetary cents before threshold comparison, avoiding floating-point rejection at an exact threshold; thresholds and coefficients are unchanged.

The UI distinguishes actual awarded level from qualification awaiting a checkpoint. Career cards, report guidance and goal bars use shared eligibility. Mixed portfolios explain exclusions while ordinary portfolio books still include every property.

Completion retains existing acknowledgement and pause semantics. Expandable rescue history keeps earned goals distinct from current usable/ready/occupied counts. Existing operations, readiness and career sections are linked. Generic lessons can be started, rendered and completed after handoff; Maple-only lessons remain unavailable. Stale unavailable lessons do not tick.

No changes to data.js, economics.js, finance.js, market behavior, acquisition pricing, borrowing terms, reserves, construction or save schema. Existing earned perks can change operations when legitimately unlocked.
