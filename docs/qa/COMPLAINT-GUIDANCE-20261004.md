## Player behavior

Operate → Customer feedback provides persistent read-only explanations. Tapping a complaint bubble opens the same sheet. Reports identify the reported building/target, floor, coordinates, category, timestamp, requested product where relevant, cause and supported remedy. View reported location moves the camera to the correct floor and selects the surviving target; Review opens Build, Operate, Business or Growth without spending. Reports are historical observations, not live fault alarms. All original response choices, prices, consequences, expiry and automation remain unchanged.

Loading-full now records reachable occupied bay count and whether overflow was available at report time. It is congestion, not automatic route failure: wait for turnover, then consider additional vehicle-connected Loading Zone near the destination if repeated. Canopies and hiring do not add loading capacity. The actual overflow algorithm is unchanged. Missing restrooms mean no working restroom in the destination building, so advice checks construction, power and water before building another. Full occupancy and reserved units are distinguished from unready inventory, blocked access and product mismatch. Normal shopping and move-outs do not imply a maintenance defect.
